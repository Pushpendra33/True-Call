Security Gap Analysis

Why Existing Enterprise Protections Fail

Deepfake attacks bypass standard IT and banking defenses.

❌ Passkeys & FIDO2 Security Keys

What they do: Prove you are logging into the correct domain.

Why they fail: They authenticate a login session, not the specific $250,000 amount or beneficiary account number.

❌ Bank Confirmation SMS / 2FA

What they do: Confirms the payment with the corporate bank.

Why they fail: The bank sees a legitimate, authorized employee making the payment. The fraud happens inside the company beforehand.

❌ Voice Call-Back Procedures

What they do: Employee phones the CFO back to confirm.

Why they fail: Voice cloning models (ElevenLabs, etc.) reproduce executive voices in real-time over telephone lines.

✅ TrueCall on Passport Prime

What it does: Checks the person, the exact amount, and the destination IBAN together on an uncompromisable physical screen.

Why it succeeds: Keys never leave Prime's secure element. What you see is what you sign.

Judges Q&A Defense

Anticipating Hard Questions

Clear answers rooted in enterprise threat models.

"What if an attacker enrolls their own key?"

Enrolment is the root of trust. TrueCall enforces in-person face-to-face enrolment by a security officer. The approver directory itself is cryptographically signed by the corporate root admin key.

"Can QR codes be scanned over a video call screen share?"

Yes. TrueCall uses compact, high-contrast QR encoding. Passport Prime's camera reliably reads QRs from laptop screens and video calls across standard 720p streams.

"Why not an iPhone or Android authenticator app?"

Mobile phones are connected to AI apps, cellular networks, browsers, and zero-day spyware (Pegasus). Prime is dedicated Human Authority Hardware that doesn't browse the web or run untrusted code.

"What happens if an executive is physically coerced?"

Physical coercion is out of scope for digital fraud defenses. However, high-value payments ($200k+) mandate dual-signers across separate executive devices.
